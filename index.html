PATIENT EDUCATION

Take Care of Your Smile

Good oral hygiene helps protect your teeth and gums. Follow these simple habits every day.

✓ Brush Twice Daily

Brush your teeth for two minutes using fluoride toothpaste.

✓ Floss Every Day

Clean between your teeth to remove plaque and food particles.

✓ Healthy Eating

Eat nutritious foods and reduce sugary snacks.

✓ Regular Checkups

Visit a dental professional regularly for preventive care.
